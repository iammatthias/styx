## Banned in current rotation

<!-- Words/phrases that creep in and get flagged. Grouped by tell. Refresh quarterly via /reflector. /voice-check reads this list. -->

**Marketing-ese** — delve, leverage (as a verb), unlock, supercharge, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, game-changing, game-changer, in today's fast-paced world, excited to share.

**Throat-clearing openers** — "Here's the thing:", "Here's why X", "The uncomfortable truth is", "It turns out", "The real X is", "Let me be clear", "The truth is", "I'm going to be honest", "Can we talk about", "It's important to note", "It's worth noting".

**Emphasis crutches** — "Full stop.", "Period.", "Let that sink in.", "Make no mistake", "This matters because", "Here's why that matters".

**Business jargon (→ replace)** — navigate the landscape → handle/address; unpack → explain; lean into → accept; deep dive → analysis; double down → commit; take a step back → reconsider; moving forward → next; circle back → revisit; on the same page → aligned; at the end of the day → cut.

**Filler adverbs** — really, just, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, actually, truly, fundamentally, inherently, inevitably, crucially, importantly. (Cut, don't soften.)

**Meta-commentary** — "Hint:", "Plot twist:", "Spoiler:", "You already know this, but", "Let me walk you through", "In this section we'll", "As we'll see", "I want to explore".

**Vague declaratives** — "The implications are significant", "The stakes are high", "The reasons are structural", "This is the deepest problem", "the consequences are real". (Show the specific, or cut.)
